# Instagram: the two recruitment posts

Both posts recruit MVHS students, not families. The student flier and the
website already speak to parents; this is the only piece aimed at the people
who would actually mentor or work fair day.

Artwork is generated by `src/build-instagram.mjs`. See [README](README.md).

**Location tag:** Mountain View, California
**Alt text:** Cream poster reading "Be a research mentor", ringed by hand drawn
science doodles with eyes, with the fair's four project categories and a QR

**Do not say** that mentors help pick the question. They do not. The student
owns the question and the project; the mentor guides the scientific method,
helps them stay organized, and encourages them. That is what /the-process and
/volunteer both promise, and the mentor conduct rules turn on it.

**Form matches the post, as of 2026-09-01.** The post advertises three shifts,
so the Event-Day Volunteering Form was given a question that can accept them:
"Which shifts can you help with?", a required checkbox offering setup the
evening before (Friday, September 25, 2026), fair day itself, and pack down
right after. The form description says the same. Before that change the form
asked only about Saturday 9 AM to 12 PM and a volunteer who wanted the Friday
shift had nowhere to say so. It had no responses yet, so nothing was disturbed.

## Reach notes

These are judgement calls, not measured results. Nothing here has been A/B
tested against this account.

- **First line is the whole caption** for most people. Instagram truncates at
  roughly 125 characters, so both hooks are one short sentence that works alone.
- **The hashtag mix is deliberate:** local place tags (`#mountainview`,
  `#losaltos`) reach people who could actually turn up, and are small enough
  that a post does not vanish instantly. Broad tags like `#stem` are there for
  topical classification, not for reach. Fifteen is a middle setting; Instagram
  has publicly suggested three to five, but a small local account is trying to
  be findable, not to rank.
- **Location tag matters more than any hashtag here.** The audience is people
  within a few miles.
- **Stories are where the students are.** Post the feed post, then immediately
  share it to your story so it reaches followers who never scroll the grid.
  The 9:16 files exist so the story is a real design rather than a shrunken
  post floating on a coloured background.
- **Add a link sticker to the story** pointing at
  `https://mvsciencefair.vercel.app/volunteer`. That is the only tappable link
  Instagram gives you outside the bio, and it converts far better than asking
  someone to retype a URL.
- **Check the bio link** before posting. If it does not already point at the
  site, set it to `https://mvsciencefair.vercel.app/volunteer` while these two
  posts are live.
- **Timing:** after school on a weekday, roughly 3 to 5 PM, or 7 to 9 PM. Avoid
  Saturday morning, which is when the target audience is asleep.
- **Pin the event-day post** to the top of the grid as September 26 gets close.
